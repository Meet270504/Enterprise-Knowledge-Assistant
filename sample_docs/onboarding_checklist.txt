Contoso North — New Hire Onboarding Checklist
Document ID: HR-ONB-2024-05
Department: Human Resources
Category: Onboarding
Classification: Fictional sample for portfolio demo

Day 1
- Collect laptop and badge from IT at the lobby desk or via remote shipment tracking link
- Complete security awareness training module (required before production system access)
- Set up MFA on email and VPN
- Meet your manager for role expectations and 30/60/90 goals

Week 1
- Join the weekly team standup calendar invite
- Read the Information Security Policy and Remote Work Policy
- Confirm emergency contact and direct deposit in the HR portal
- Shadow one customer or internal meeting with your onboarding buddy

First 30 Days
- Complete role-specific training path assigned in Learning Hub
- Submit first weekly status note to your manager
- If remote-eligible, submit home office stipend request with receipts up to $400

Notes
Managers must schedule a 30-day check-in. Access to Confidential data systems is granted only after security training is marked complete.
