# Contoso North — Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

**Document ID:** HR-RWP-2024-02  
**Department:** Human Resources  
**Category:** Workplace  
**Effective:** February 15, 2024  
**Classification:** Fictional sample for portfolio demo — not a real company policy

## Work Arrangements
- Office-primary: on site at least 4 days per week
- Hybrid: on site Tuesday and Thursday, remote the remaining workdays unless a customer meeting requires otherwise
- Remote-eligible: fully remote with manager and HR approval for roles designated remote-eligible

## Core Collaboration Hours
Regardless of location, employees must be reachable on chat and email between 10:00 and 15:00 in their team's primary time zone, Monday through Friday, excluding approved PTO and holidays.

## Equipment
Remote and hybrid employees receive one company laptop, headset, and a $400 one-time home office stipend during their first 90 days. Contoso North does not reimburse recurring home internet for hybrid employees; fully remote employees may claim up to $50/month for internet with receipts.

## Workspace Expectations
Remote workspaces must allow confidential calls without exposing screens to the public. Working from cafes or shared coworking spaces is allowed for Internal data only; Confidential or Restricted data requires a private space and VPN.

## Travel to Office
Fully remote employees may be asked to travel to a Contoso North office up to four times per year for planning weeks. Approved travel follows the Travel & Expense Policy.
