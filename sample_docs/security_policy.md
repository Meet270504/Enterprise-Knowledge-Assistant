# Contoso North — Information Security Policy

**Document ID:** SEC-ISP-2024-03  
**Department:** Security  
**Category:** Security  
**Effective:** March 1, 2024  
**Classification:** Fictional sample for portfolio demo — not a real company policy

## Purpose
This policy protects Contoso North systems, customer data, and intellectual property.

## Password and Authentication
- Passwords must be at least 14 characters and unique to Contoso North accounts.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for email, VPN, cloud admin consoles, and production systems.
- Sharing passwords or MFA codes is prohibited. Password managers approved by IT are recommended.

## Device Security
Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and the company endpoint protection agent. Personal devices may access email only through the approved mobile management profile. Unmanaged personal computers may not download customer data.

## Data Classification
- Public: marketing website content
- Internal: ordinary business documents
- Confidential: customer PII, contracts, financials
- Restricted: production credentials, encryption keys, security findings

Confidential and Restricted data must not be stored in personal cloud drives or sent to personal email.

## Incident Reporting
Report suspected phishing, malware, or data loss to security@contoso-north.example within 1 hour of discovery. Do not attempt to "test" production systems without written authorization from the Security team.

## Third-Party Tools
Installing unsanctioned browser extensions or shadow SaaS tools that process company data is not allowed. Request a security review through the IT service portal before adopting a new vendor that stores Contoso North data.
