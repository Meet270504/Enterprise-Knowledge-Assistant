# Contoso North — Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

**Document ID:** HR-PTO-2024-01  
**Department:** Human Resources  
**Category:** Benefits  
**Effective:** January 1, 2024  
**Classification:** Fictional sample for portfolio demo — not a real company policy

## Eligibility
Full-time employees accrue PTO from their start date. Part-time employees who work at least 20 hours per week accrue PTO on a pro-rated basis.

## Accrual Rates
- Years 0–2: 15 days (120 hours) per calendar year
- Years 3–5: 20 days (160 hours) per calendar year
- Years 6+: 25 days (200 hours) per calendar year

Accrual is calculated per pay period. Unused PTO may carry over up to 40 hours into the next calendar year. Accrual pauses during unpaid leaves longer than 30 consecutive days.

## Requesting Time Off
Submit PTO requests in the Contoso North HR portal at least 10 business days before the first day of leave when the absence is longer than two consecutive days. Managers should respond within 3 business days. Blackout periods may apply during fiscal close (last two weeks of each quarter) unless pre-approved by a director.

## Illness and Same-Day Absences
For unexpected illness, notify your manager before your scheduled start time when possible. Absences of three or more consecutive workdays may require a doctor's note.

## Payout on Separation
Employees who resign with at least two weeks' notice receive payout of unused accrued PTO up to 80 hours. Employees terminated for cause are not eligible for PTO payout.
