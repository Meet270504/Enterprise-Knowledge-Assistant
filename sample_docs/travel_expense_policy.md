# Contoso North — Travel and Expense Policy

**Document ID:** FIN-TNE-2024-04  
**Department:** Finance  
**Category:** Expenses  
**Effective:** April 1, 2024  
**Classification:** Fictional sample for portfolio demo — not a real company policy

## Pre-Approval
Domestic trips estimated over $1,000 and all international trips require manager pre-approval in the expense system before booking. Sales visits under $1,000 may be booked without director approval if within quarterly travel budget.

## Air Travel
Book economy class for flights under 6 hours. Business class may be requested for flights longer than 6 hours with director approval. Prefer refundable fares only when itinerary changes are likely.

## Lodging
Hotel rates should not exceed $220/night in most U.S. cities or $300/night in New York, San Francisco, or Seattle unless no reasonable alternative exists. Airbnb or similar is allowed when total cost is lower and a receipt is provided.

## Meals
Daily meal allowance is $75 domestically and $95 internationally, including tips. Alcohol is not reimbursable except for pre-approved customer entertainment capped at $40 per guest with attendee names listed.

## Ground Transportation
Rideshare and taxis are reimbursable to/from airports and customer sites. Rental cars require manager approval. Parking at the home airport is reimbursable up to 7 days per trip.

## Submission Deadlines
Submit expense reports within 14 days of trip completion. Receipts are required for any expense of $25 or more. Late submissions after 60 days need Finance director exception approval.
